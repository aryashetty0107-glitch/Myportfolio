Celebrating an inspiring legacy

Puneeth Rajkumar

A special inspiration spotlight

Greatness is measured by the impact we leave.

Puneeth Rajkumar (1975–2021), fondly known as Appu, was a celebrated Kannada actor, producer and philanthropist. His warmth, dedication and contributions to society continue to inspire people across Karnataka.

At TECHNOVA 2026, we honour his legacy as a reminder that talent becomes truly meaningful when it is paired with kindness, purpose and service.

This is a tribute feature. Puneeth Rajkumar is not a live guest speaker at the 2026 event.
